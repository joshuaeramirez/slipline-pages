What SlipLine does

SlipLine shows how committed dates in a Jira project have moved over time, using Jira's own change history. It runs entirely on Atlassian's Forge platform.

What it reads from your Jira site

With read-only permissions, for projects the app can see: each issue's key, summary, issue type, creation date, status, due date, start date, target start and end, sprint, story points, parent and issue links; whether the issue has a security level; the change history of those fields; the site's field and status definitions; and the project's board and sprints. It cannot create, edit or delete anything in Jira.

What it stores

In Atlassian-hosted Forge storage belonging to your site's installation: for each issue, its key, summary, type, creation date and the values of the fields above at creation; each change to those fields (which field, old and new value, and when); and weekly summaries computed from them.

What it does not store

  • Account IDs, names, email addresses or avatars of any person. Who changed a date is not stored.
  • Issue descriptions, comments or attachments.
  • Issues with a security level. These are never stored or shown; the app shows only how many were left out.

Who made a change

The issue Date history panel shows who made each date change. That name is read live from Jira when the panel is opened, shown only to people who can already see the issue, and never stored.

Where your data goes

Nowhere outside Atlassian. The app makes no calls to any server outside Atlassian. The publisher operates no servers for it and cannot read your stored data. App logs hold only counts, timings and internal run identifiers (which include the project key), never issue keys, summaries, dates or names. Your site administrator controls whether those logs are shared with the publisher.

Deleting your data

Uninstall SlipLine from your site. Atlassian then soft-deletes the app's stored data and destroys it at the end of the retention period described in Atlassian's documentation, Data lifecycle for Forge-hosted storage. That page also notes that a reinstall is a new installation, which can be relinked to the old data only if requested within 21 days of uninstalling. To ask about your data, write to neuralplan@nbpmi.com.
